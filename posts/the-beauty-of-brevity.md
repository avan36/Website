---
title: The beauty of brevity
date: 2026-11-02
description: ' Why AI makes it important to write briefly.'
---

Writing a lot used to be a sign of effort – more words equaled more time spent. Now, with AI, more words are a sign of sloppiness. I notice this myself when I’m working on my app. Because of Claude’s proclivity for endlessly generating more text, any project naturally accumulates more and more text – every button ends up having an explanation and every screen has just a little too much text. The most beautiful thing about being human is our need to make trade-offs about what to create. Unlike AI, we don’t have unlimited willpower or time to create stuff, so we end up having to choose what to work on or what to write. Writing a lot used to show effort. Now the effort is in what you leave out.
