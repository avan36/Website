title: Is my app vibe-coded?
date: 2026-11-07
description: A reflection on whether my app, middle place, is vibe-coded
---

At what point do I call my app vibe-coded? I’ve found that an awkward situation when explaining middle place to my friends. It’s an app that lets you journal and have your journal write back to you with what it noticed. I’ve put a lot of care into every feature. And every decision has been mine – how do I ensure privacy of people using the app? How do I make sure people don’t overload the server with AI calls?

It's a free app that syncs end-to-end encrypted and uses AI both to respond to you and jot down the stuff that's important to you: the people in your life, your goals, trips, life events, and hangouts. It also lets you see your life as a story – so my 400k journal is no longer an opaque block of text but is interactive instead. If you add friends, it also can notify them about stuff going on in your life, which I've found really helps me stay connected with people from Wesleyan.

My favourite part is that you just have to journal like normal, and middle place takes notes all by itself. Last week my friend told me, "middle place has given me the vocabulary to talk about feelings I didn't have the words for."

And yet, it’s something I could never have built all by myself. Is it vibe-coded? My ego would say no. The other half would say it was a collaboration. The decisions were mine, and the decisions are the product.
