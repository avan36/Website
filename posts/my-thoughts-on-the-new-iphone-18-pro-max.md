---
title: My thoughts on the iPhone 18 Pro Event
date: 2026-09-22
description: My thoughts on the new iPhone 18 Pro
---

**My favorite parts are the following:**

- The A20 Pro chip – it seems so much faster
- The next-generation vapor chamber cooling – this seems like a genuine upgrade over my iPhone 17 Pro Max
- The new Photographic Styles – do these really have to be limited to the new iPhone 18 Pro models? I don't see any reason for limiting them other than just wanting us to upgrade
- Showing three Live Activities at once on the Dynamic Island seems awesome
- Apple Reference Image – Apple's new verification that a photo is 100% real and not AI-generated – seems awesome, although it's so technically complicated and fiddly that it's unclear if everyday people would actually use it. This seems more like something that's going to appeal to 1% of people.
- Better battery life – my iPhone 17 Pro Max battery dies so quickly, although I'm not sure if this is going to be a big enough increase to assuage my battery anxiety

**I'm not as excited about the following:**

- The variable aperture lens – what's the point of this on such a small camera sensor? This feels more like a gimmick to me, although seeing it open and close in person is awesome.
- The Qualcomm modem in the US Pro Max – the C2 is supposed to be more power-efficient. It's a shame the US Pro Max is stuck with the same Qualcomm modem as last year, while the 18 Pro and the rest of the world get the C2.
- The colors are nice but not as nice as last year's – I still love my orange iPhone 17 Pro Max, which I've started using without a case now that it's older and I'm not as worried about dropping it.
