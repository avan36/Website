Questions

Good to know.

What is busy beer?

busy beer is a free drink rating app for iPhone. You rate beer, wine, cider, cocktails, sake and spirits, it maps your palate, and its menu scanner ranks every drink on a menu by how much you're predicted to like it.

Is busy beer free?

Yes. It's a free download on the App Store with no subscription, no in-app purchases and no ads.

How does the menu scanner work?

Point your camera at a drink menu, tap list or wine list, or choose photos. busy beer reads every drink, classifies it and ranks the whole list by predicted match, with a short reason for each and a top pick. Drinks you've already rated are pinned to your own score.

Can I rate wine, cocktails and spirits, not just beer?

Yes. busy beer covers six categories: beer, wine, cider, cocktails, sake and spirits, each with its own flavor tags and taste profile. It can also predict your taste in a new category from the ones it knows, with a confidence rating.

Is there a public feed like Untappd?

No. You add friends by @handle or friend code and see what they've been drinking, and Taste Match compares your palates. There's no public feed and no strangers.

Is busy beer on Android?

No. busy beer is iPhone only for now, and needs iOS 26 or later.
