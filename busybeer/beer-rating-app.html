What a beer rating app is for

A beer rating app is a diary for what you drink. You log a beer, give it a score, and over time you build a record you can check before you buy the same can twice. That part is simple, and plenty of apps do it.

The harder question is the one you have at the bar: of the twelve things on this board, which will I actually like? A list of past scores doesn't answer it. Knowing the shape of your taste does. That's what busy beer is built around.

How rating works

Find a drink in the catalog, or add it if it's missing, and score it from 1 to 10 in half steps. Tag the flavors you noticed and add a note if you want to remember where you had it. Each category has its own tags: for beer that's hoppy, malty, sour, bitter, sweet and roasty; for wine it's dry, tannic, acidic, fruity, oaky and body.

  • Rate from the lock screen. Tap a drink you've ordered and a reminder an hour later lets you rate it straight from the notification.
  • No signal, no problem. Ratings save offline and sync when you're back online.
  • See how it lands for everyone. Each drink shows the community average next to your own score.

Not sure what a 6 means compared with an 8? The guide to how to rate beer has a simple scale and a tasting routine you can use in under a minute.

Your palate, mapped

After a handful of ratings, busy beer builds a taste profile for each category. For beer, that's six flavor axes from 0 to 100 (hoppy, malty, sour, bitter, sweet, roasty), the styles you keep coming back to, and whether you lean toward sessionable or strong. It's written in plain language, so you can say "I like crisp, bitter, not too sweet" at a bar and mean it.

If you're not sure where you sit, beer styles, explained by taste lays out the main families by the flavors you'll actually notice.

Scan the menu before you order

Point your camera at a drink menu, a tap list or a wine list. busy beer reads every drink on it, works out what each one is, and ranks the whole list by how much you're predicted to like it. Drinks you've already rated are pinned to your own score. You'll see prices where the menu lists them, you can ask questions about the menu, and past scans are saved so you can look back at what was on offer. More in the drink menu scanner guide.

Beyond beer

Know your beer taste but lost on a wine list? busy beer translates the palate it already knows into the other five categories, and tells you how confident it is, so you can tell a strong match from an educated guess. Wine for beer drinkers explains the overlaps it looks for.

Friends, not a feed

Add friends by their @handle or a friend code and you'll see what they've been drinking. Taste Match compares two palates: a match score, the flavors you share, drinks you both loved, and the one they'd pick for you that you haven't tried. There's no public feed, no strangers and no algorithm deciding what you see. If you're coming from a check-in app, the Untappd alternative guide covers what's the same and what isn't.

Your year, told as a story

Taste, distilled is a tap-through recap of your drinking year: your top category, your flavor fingerprint, your signature pour and your scoring persona. Any slide can be shared as a card.

Private by default

You sign in with Apple, so there's no password, and Hide My Email works. There are no ads and no third-party trackers, and your data is never sold. Your ratings are visible to you and the friends you add, and you can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app. The privacy policy has the details.

Please enjoy responsibly. busy beer is for adults of legal drinking age. Predicted scores are estimates of taste, not facts about a drink: always confirm alcohol content, ingredients and allergens with the venue.

Questions

Is busy beer free?

Yes. It's a free download on the App Store with no subscription, no in-app purchases and no ads.

Is busy beer on Android?

No. busy beer is iPhone only for now, and needs iOS 26 or later.

Can I rate wine, cocktails and spirits too?

Yes. busy beer covers six categories: beer, wine, cider, cocktails, sake and spirits. Each one gets its own flavor tags and its own taste profile.

How is busy beer different from Untappd?

Untappd is a social check-in app built around a public feed, venues and badges. busy beer is built around your own palate: it maps what you like, predicts how much you'll like a drink before you order, and ranks whole menus for you. There is no public feed; you share with friends you add.

Does it work without signal?

Ratings save offline and sync when you're back online. The menu scanner and taste predictions need a connection.

How accurate are the predicted scores?

They're AI estimates built from your own ratings, so they get better the more you rate. Treat them as a guide to taste, not a fact about a drink, and always confirm alcohol content, ingredients and allergens with the venue.
