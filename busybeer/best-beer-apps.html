The best beer apps in 2026, compared honestly

Updated November 2026

There's no single best beer app, only the one that fits what you want from it: a social check-in, a long review, a wine-list scan, or a straight answer about what to order. Here's how the main ones compare.

Who wrote this. We make busy beer, so weigh that. Everything said here about other apps comes from their own websites, help centers and App Store or Google Play listings, checked on 14 November 2026. Prices and features change, so check before you pay for anything. Sources are listed at the end.

The short version

  • Untappd is the biggest social check-in app, with badges, venues and live tap lists. Since October 2026 it covers wine and spirits too.
  • busy beer is for knowing what you'll like before you order: a taste profile per category and a scanner that ranks a whole drink menu for you. iPhone only.
  • BeerAdvocate is the place for long-form reviews, style guides and forums. It's a website, with no app right now.
  • Vivino is the wine app: label scanning, crowd ratings and a personal match score. Wine only.
  • Distiller is for whiskey and other spirits, with expert scores and collection tracking. Spirits only.
  • RateBeer closed in February 2025. If you were a RateBeer user, see what happened to RateBeer.

Untappd

Untappd is the default beer app for a reason. You check in a beer, rate it from 0 to 5, earn badges, and see what your friends and the wider community are drinking. Venues that use Untappd for Business publish live tap lists, so you can see what's on before you go. Version 5.0, released in October 2026, renamed it "Untappd: Beer, Wine & Spirits", added wine and spirits to a catalog of nearly 10 million drinks, and removed direct messages and Facebook sign-in.

Good for: the social side, badges, finding beer at bars, and Android users. Less good for: anyone who wants a quiet, private record. Check-ins are public unless you make your whole account private. The free tier shows ads, and rating in tenths of a point needs Insiders (free accounts rate in quarter steps).

busy beer

busy beer is built around one question: of everything on this menu, what will I like? You rate drinks from 1 to 10 in half steps with flavor tags, and it builds a taste profile for each of six categories. Point your camera at a drink menu, tap list or wine list and it ranks every drink on it for you, with a short reason for each. It can also translate your beer taste into wine or cocktails, with a confidence rating. Friends are added by handle or friend code, and there's no public feed. More in a beer rating app that learns what you like.

Good for: deciding what to order, people who drink more than beer, and anyone who wants no ads and no feed. Less good for: Android users, badge collectors, and finding which bar near you has a beer on tap. It's a young app with a smaller catalog, and it can't import an Untappd history.

BeerAdvocate

BeerAdvocate has been around since the 1990s and is back in its founder's hands after he bought it from Untappd's parent company in July 2026. Ratings break a beer into look, smell, taste, feel and overall, each from 1 to 5, and each beer gets a 0 to 100 BA Score once it has enough ratings. Its forums, style guides and long reviews are its strength.

Good for: detailed reviews, beer trading and talking beer with enthusiasts. Less good for: use at the bar. There's no app at the moment (it works in a phone browser), no menu scanning and no personal recommendations that we could find.

Vivino

If you mostly drink wine, Vivino is the app to beat. Scan a label to see crowd ratings, reviews and food pairings. Its "Match for You" score estimates how likely you are to rate a wine 4 stars or more, once you've rated a handful. Scanning a whole restaurant wine list is listed as a Premium feature, and Vivino is also a store, so expect to be offered bottles to buy.

Good for: buying and choosing wine. Less good for: beer, cocktails or anything that isn't wine.

Distiller

Distiller covers whiskey, bourbon, scotch, tequila, rum, gin and other spirits, with expert tasting scores from 0 to 100 next to user ratings. Pro adds bottle values for collectors, flavor search and an ad-free app. The in-app "Pick For Me" chooses from your own lists rather than recommending new bottles.

Good for: whiskey drinkers and collectors. Less good for: beer and wine, which it doesn't cover.

What happened to RateBeer?

RateBeer stopped operating on 1 February 2025 and went fully offline two days later. It had been owned by AB InBev since 2019. Users were given a deadline to export their ratings, and the site no longer loads. If you were a RateBeer regular, the closest replacements are BeerAdvocate for detailed reviews and Untappd for a large community. If you mostly want to remember what you liked and get better at ordering, busy beer is built for that.

Which one should you pick?

  • You want to share every beer and collect badges: Untappd.
  • You want to know what to order from the menu in front of you: busy beer.
  • You write long reviews and live on forums: BeerAdvocate.
  • You mostly drink wine: Vivino. If you drink wine and beer, busy beer covers both with one taste profile each. See wine for beer drinkers.
  • You mostly drink whiskey: Distiller.
  • You're on Android: Untappd, Vivino or Distiller. busy beer is iPhone only.

Plenty of people use two. A common pairing is Untappd for check-ins with friends and busy beer at the bar to rank the menu. There's more on that in an Untappd alternative built around your taste.

Please enjoy responsibly. Every app here is for adults of legal drinking age. A rating app is for remembering what you liked, not a reason to drink more.

Questions

What is the best app for rating beer?

It depends on what you want from it. Untappd is best for social check-ins and badges, BeerAdvocate for detailed reviews, and busy beer for learning your taste and ranking a drink menu before you order.

Is there a Vivino for beer?

Untappd is the closest match for crowd ratings of beer. If you want what Vivino's Match for You score does, a personal prediction of how much you'll like something, busy beer does that for beer, wine, cider, cocktails, sake and spirits, and ranks whole menus too.

Does RateBeer still exist?

No. RateBeer stopped operating on 1 February 2025 and went fully offline on 3 February 2025. BeerAdvocate and Untappd are the closest replacements.

Do people still use Untappd?

Yes. It's still actively developed: version 5.0 in October 2026 redesigned the app and added wine and spirits, and its App Store listing has more than 200,000 ratings.

Is there a free beer rating app without ads?

busy beer is free with no ads, no subscription and no in-app purchases. The free tiers of Untappd, BeerAdvocate and Distiller show ads, which their paid plans remove.

Which beer apps work on Android?

Untappd works on Android, as do Vivino for wine and Distiller for spirits. BeerAdvocate works in any browser. busy beer is iPhone only.
