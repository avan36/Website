An Untappd alternative built around your taste

Updated November 2026

Untappd is great at checking in, badges and seeing what's on tap. If you'd rather have a private record of what you like and a straight answer about what to order next, here's how busy beer compares.

Who wrote this. We make busy beer, so weigh that. Facts about Untappd come from its own App Store listing, help center and announcements, checked on 14 November 2026. Sources are at the end.

What Untappd does well

It's worth being fair first. Untappd has the biggest drinking community there is, badges people genuinely chase, and live tap lists from venues that publish them, so you can see what's pouring before you go. It runs on iPhone, iPad, Android and the web. Version 5.0, in October 2026, redesigned the app, added wine and spirits to a catalog of nearly 10 million drinks, and added personalized recommendations for drinks, producers and venues. If you love checking in and sharing, it's hard to beat.

Why people look for an alternative

  • It's public by default. Check-ins go to a community feed unless you make your whole account private, which limits them to friends.
  • Ads. The free tier shows ads. Insiders, at $5.99 a month or $54.99 a year, cuts them to "very limited".
  • Finer ratings cost extra. Free accounts rate from 0 to 5 in quarter steps. Tenths need Insiders.
  • The menu is still on you. Untappd can show a venue's published menu, but it doesn't read a photo of the board in front of you and rank it for your taste.
  • Some people just want a quieter app. A private record of what they liked, without badges, toasts or a feed.

What changes when the app is built around your taste

In busy beer, every rating feeds a profile instead of a feed. Rate a few beers and it maps where you sit on hoppy, malty, sour, bitter, sweet and roasty, which styles you keep coming back to, and how strong you like them. That profile is what it uses at the bar: photograph the tap list and every beer comes back ranked by how much you're predicted to like it, with a short reason and a top pick. Beers you've already rated are pinned to your own score. Details are in the drink menu scanner guide.

Because the profile is per category, it also works when you're handed a wine list or a cocktail menu. busy beer translates the taste it knows into the new category and says how confident it is. Friends still have a place: add them by @handle or friend code, see what they've been drinking, and use Taste Match to compare palates.

What you'd give up

  • Your Untappd history. There's no import, so you start fresh. A handful of ratings is enough for a first profile, and it sharpens as you go.
  • Badges, venues and live tap lists. busy beer has none of these.
  • Android and the web. busy beer is iPhone only, on iOS 26 or later.
  • Size. It's a young app with a much smaller community and catalog. You can add a drink that's missing.

Or use both

You don't have to choose. Plenty of people keep Untappd for checking in with friends and open busy beer when they're staring at a board of twenty beers. One is for sharing what you drank, the other for deciding what to drink.

Other Untappd alternatives

  • BeerAdvocate: detailed reviews, style guides and forums. It's a website for now, with no app, and free accounts see ads.
  • Vivino: if it's wine you're after, with label scanning and a personal match score. Wine only.
  • Distiller: the equivalent for whiskey and other spirits.
  • RateBeer: closed in February 2025, so it's no longer an option.

Questions

Is there an Untappd alternative without ads?

Yes. busy beer is free with no ads, no subscription and no in-app purchases. It's iPhone only.

Is Untappd only for beer?

Not anymore. Version 5.0, released in October 2026, added wine and spirits, and Untappd also covers cider, ready-to-drink cocktails and non-alcoholic drinks. A setting keeps it beer-only if you prefer.

Can I make my Untappd account private?

Yes. Untappd's Make Account Private setting shows your check-ins to friends only and removes them from public drink, producer and venue pages.

Can I import my Untappd history into busy beer?

Not at the moment. You start fresh, and a handful of ratings is enough for a first taste profile.

Is there a wine equivalent to Untappd?

Vivino is the biggest wine app, and Untappd itself now covers wine. busy beer rates wine alongside beer and keeps a separate taste profile for each.

Can I use busy beer and Untappd together?

Yes. Many people check in on Untappd and use busy beer to rank a drink menu before they order.
